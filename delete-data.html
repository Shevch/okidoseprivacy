OkiDose — delete your data

App: OkiDose (Google Play, package app.pilly). Developer: Ilia Idelman.

OkiDose has no user accounts. Your medications, schedule, history, notes and photos are stored only on your phone and never reach our servers. The only data that leaves the phone belongs to the optional Family alert feature.

Delete your cloud data (Family alert)

  1. Open OkiDose.
  2. Go to Settings → Data and privacy.
  3. Tap Delete my cloud data and confirm.

This deletes at once: your anonymous Firebase ID, your name as shown to family members, the links to your family members and their push tokens.

What is kept and for how long

  • Missed-dose events are deleted right after they are delivered — they are never kept.
  • A daily counter of family alerts (anonymous ID, date, number of alerts; no name and no medication) is deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; we do not store payment data.

Delete the data on your phone

Uninstall OkiDose, or open Android Settings → Apps → OkiDose → Storage → Clear data. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you put them.

Can't open the app?

Write to shevch@gmail.com with the subject “OkiDose data deletion”. We will delete the cloud data within 30 days.
