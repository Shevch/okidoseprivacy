Delete your OkiDose data

OkiDose: Pill Reminder · developer Ilia Idelman

OkiDose has no user accounts. Your medications, schedule, dose history, notes and photos are stored only on your phone and never reach our servers. The only data that leaves the phone belongs to the optional Family alert feature.

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

  1. Open OkiDose.
  2. Go to Settings → Data and privacy.
  3. Tap Delete my cloud data and confirm.
This deletes everything OkiDose stored in the cloud and turns off Family alert. Data on your phone stays.

Option 2 — by e-mail

Write to shevch@gmail.com with the subject “OkiDose data deletion”. If you can, include the user id from Settings → About. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

  • The anonymous Family alert ID and push-notification token of your phone.
  • The name you entered for Family alert.
  • Links to the family members who receive your alerts.
  • Any missed-dose events not yet delivered.

What is kept

  • Nothing in the cloud. Missed-dose events are deleted automatically as soon as they are delivered.
  • Daily alert counters (only the anonymous ID, a date and a number; no name or medication) used to limit alerts. After your ID is deleted they are no longer linked to anything.
  • Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play; manage or cancel them in Google Play.
  • Data on your phone: delete it by uninstalling OkiDose or with Android Settings → Apps → OkiDose → Storage → Clear data.

See also the privacy policy.
